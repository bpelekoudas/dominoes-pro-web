# Store plan

This is a proposal for Bryan to look at. The store on this branch is a preview. The coins are a sample balance of 2,450. Nothing is saved on the server, and nothing can be charged.

## How a player earns coins

Coins come from playing, not from a credit card.

- Finish a game: 25 coins.
- Win that game: 40 more, so a win is 65.
- Score an UH-OH (a single play of 20 or more): 15. Once per hand.
- First game of a day: 50.
- Climb a season tier: 200, once for that tier.

Someone who plays one game a day and wins about half of them lands near 80 to 100 coins a day. A quiet week is a few hundred coins. A busy week can clear an everyday item.

## What things cost

Free, and already in the collection: the house character, Barista, Golfer, ivory tiles, classic green felt, brand-red felt, a plain frame, and the glass shatter.

Everyday, 400 to 900 coins: Frat, Fisherman, Retired, Hipster, Rocker, Florida Man, Lumberjack, wood tiles, a gold ring, red enamel, Neon.

Standout, 1,000 to 1,600 coins: Cowboy, Gold tiles, Marble, City night, Desert, Smoked charcoal tiles, Florida beach, Fire.

Signature, 1,800 to 2,400 coins: 2 Hot, Money blast, Mogul.

At 65 coins for a win, Neon (900) is about two weeks of daily play. Mogul (2,400) is a month of showing up, or less if the daily bonus and UH-OH coins are in the mix. Prices can move after a playtest. The ranges are the part to keep: a week for a small look, a month for a loud one.

## Season rewards

These are not for sale. The preview treats the player as a Hustler, so Hustler rewards are already owned and higher ones stay locked.

- Hustler: Tailgate table, Hustler frame.
- Shark: the pirate character, Shark frame, Lightning.
- UH-OH Legend: Scarface, Legend frame.

Rookie frame is owned because everyone starts there.

## Real money

The coin chip's plus opens a drawing of three packs: a Handful (500 coins, $0.99), a Stack (1,200, $1.99), and a Vault (3,000, $3.99). The buy button says "Not for sale." No pack can be purchased. Those prices are a sketch of a later conversation, not a price list.

## What this preview does not do

Buying and equipping last only until the store is closed. Ranked games, guest accounts, and the live table are unchanged. A tile skin and a table scene can be previewed. They do not change a real game yet.
