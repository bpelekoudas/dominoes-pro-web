# Store plan

Bryan approved this economy. The store on this branch is still a preview. The sample wallet is 1,840 Bones and 180 Gold Bones. Nothing is saved on the server, and nothing can be charged.

## Two currencies

Bones are the currency you earn by playing. You can also get Bones by turning Gold Bones into them.

Gold Bones are the real-money currency. They buy premium looks only. Those looks are cosmetic, listed in the store, and some are limited. Gold Bones can become Bones. Bones cannot become Gold Bones. Neither currency pays out as cash.

100 Gold Bones become 500 Bones. That is the only conversion, and it goes one way.

## How Bones are earned

- Finish a game: 20 Bones.
- Win that game: 40 more, so a win is 60.
- Each UH-OH (a single play of 20 or more): 10.
- Daily login: 50, rising to 150 on a 7-day streak.
- Daily challenge: 100.
- Games themselves cap at about 600 Bones a day. Login, the streak, and the daily challenge sit outside that cap.

A casual day lands around 250 to 350 Bones. One example: three games and one win is 100 from play, plus a 50 login and a 100 challenge, which is 250. A busier day with the streak climbed sits nearer 350. Across a month that is about 8,000 to 10,000 Bones.

## What things cost

Rarity is the price band for Bones. The border and the label on each card use the same colors: Common is soft white, Rare is blue, Epic is purple, Legendary is gold.

- Common, 500 Bones. About two days.
- Rare, 2,500 Bones. About one to two weeks.
- Epic, 10,000 Bones. About a month.
- Legendary, 30,000 to 50,000 Bones. About three to six months. The Racer in this preview is 35,000.

Included with the game, already in the sample collection: UH-OH, Barista, Golfer, ivory tiles, classic green, brand red, a plain frame, and the glass shatter.

Bones prices in the preview: Frat, Fisherman, Hipster, Retired, Neon, Wood, and the gold ring are Common at 500. Florida Man, Rocker, Lumberjack, Gold tiles, Marble, Desert, and Fire are Rare at 2,500. Cowboy, City night, and Smoked tiles are Epic at 10,000. Racer is Legendary at 35,000.

## Three ways to get an item

Every item is one of these, and the card says which.

- Bones. The prices above.
- Gold Bones. Premium, cosmetic only, and some are limited. In this preview: Red enamel 300, Florida beach 500, 2 Hot 800, Money blast 1,200, Mogul 1,500. The band for premium looks is 300 to 1,500 Gold Bones.
- Earned only. Never sold. Season tiers, the World Series trophy, and achievements such as 100 UH-OHs. In this preview the player is a Hustler, so Rookie, Hustler, and Tailgate are already owned. Shark (the pirate, the Shark frame, Lightning), UH-OH Legend (Scarface and the Legend frame), Century (100 UH-OHs), and the World Series frame stay locked until they are earned.

## Gold Bones packs

A drawing only. The button says "Not for sale."

- $0.99 for 100 Gold Bones.
- $4.99 for 550 Gold Bones.
- $19.99 for 2,500 Gold Bones.

## Season pass idea

$4.99 for the season. Everything on it is listed up front: 1,500 Bones the day you start, the Fire effect, and the Desert table. No loot box and no paid random draw. The same rule covers the whole store. If it can be yours, you can see it before you pay.

Earned rewards stay off the pass. A season tier, a World Series win, and 100 UH-OHs are not for sale.

## What this preview does not do

Buying and equipping last only until the store is closed. Ranked games, guest accounts, and the live table are unchanged.
